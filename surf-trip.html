A reef we don’t name. A private white-sand beach, an A-frame over reef, and the only way in is by boat.

From the water

Real days on this boat

The day

Empty on purpose.

The surf day runs on the Great Marlin out of Herradura to a private white-sand beach with an A-frame reef wave. The beach is private, so the public can’t walk in: the only way to the wave is by boat, which is the whole point.

We anchor off the reef, you throw your board over the side and jump in after it. Between sessions the boat is your base: shade, food, a cold drink, and turtles under the hull.

We don’t print the wave’s name. “A reef we don’t name” is the trip. You will see it when you get there.

Six hours on the water, provisioned like the full day: lunch, beer, water and fruit aboard.

Boat entry only.

It’s a private beach. The only way to the wave is the boat, and the boat is yours for the day.

What’s included

Aboard

  • The boat and crew, your group only.
  • Six hours, out of Herradura and back.
  • Lunch, beer, water and fruit, aboard.
  • Shade, berths and a private head, below deck between sessions.

Bring

  • Your board, and whatever you surf in.
  • Sun cover, hat, sunglasses, sunscreen.
  • Seasickness medication, if boats get to you.

Not included

  • The crew tip, 15–20% of the charter is customary, in cash, if the crew earned it.
  • Transfers to the beach, easy to add through Jacó Transport, on the same booking thread.

Questions

  • Where is the wave?

    Close to Los Sueños, reachable only by boat. We keep the name off the internet so it stays the way it is.

  • How many people can come?

    The price covers up to six guests. Each guest beyond that is $40, up to ten.

  • Do I need to bring a board?

    Yes, bring your own board and whatever you surf in.

  • Can we combine surfing and fishing?

    Tell us when you book and we will tell you honestly what fits in the day.

Before you book

  • A 50% deposit holds your date.

    Pay it by Zelle, Venmo or PayPal. The details come with your confirmation, and nothing is charged until we answer.

  • The other half, in cash on the day.

    US dollars, settled on the beach before we head out.

  • Cancel 10 or more days out

    and the deposit comes back to you.

  • Cancel inside 10 days

    and the deposit is kept. The date was held for you.

  • Rain or shine, the boat runs.

    Only weather that closes the port calls off a day.

  • If the day is called off, you are covered.

    When the captain or the port closes the water for safety, we move you to another date or refund your deposit in full. Your choice.
